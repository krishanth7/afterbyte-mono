# Community expectations

Treat contributors with respect. Give specific, constructive feedback on the work. Welcome people regardless of background, experience, or identity.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not publish private information.

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate public content and restrict participation where necessary. Decisions should consider context and aim to protect a productive community.

For ordinary concerns, contact the maintainer through the public project issue tracker without including private or identifying details. This repository does not currently provide a confidential reporting channel.
